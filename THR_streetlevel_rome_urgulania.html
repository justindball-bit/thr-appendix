LEPIDA: (carefully) I'm sure there are many explanations for that.
URGULANIA: He was standing directly below your window, Lepida. Directly below it. Looking up.
LEPIDA: Then your argument is with him, not with me.
URGULANIA: Oh I've had that argument. That argument has been had. Now I'm having this one.
(a beat)
LEPIDA: I was washing my arms.
URGULANIA: My husband doesn't look up for arms, Lepida.
LEPIDA: (to someone inside, impressed despite herself) She's got a point actually.
URGULANIA: I heard that!
LEPIDA: Urgulania, I wake up at the same time every morning, I fill the same basin, I set it on the same ledge. This has been my routine since before your husband even moved to this street. I am not going to rearrange my life because he has weak character.
URGULANIA: Weak character! You're calling my husband —
LEPIDA: I'm saying a woman washing herself is not an invitation. That's a thing your husband needs to understand, not me.
(long silence)
(a man's voice from inside Urgulania's apartment, very quiet, clearly trying to go unnoticed)
URGULANIA: (without turning) Don't you dare walk past this window right now, Tiberius. Don't you dare.
(silence)
LEPIDA: Is he still there?
URGULANIA: (through her teeth) He's frozen in the middle of the room like a statue.
